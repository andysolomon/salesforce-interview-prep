Page 3 · Learning Objectives

After studying this topic, you should be able to:

  • Define the two types of org strategies.
  • Describe the different architectural, business, and technical considerations that influence the org strategy.
  • Evaluate the customer enterprise landscape and recommend suitable org strategies based on specific requirements.
Page 4 · Introduction

Introduction

In today's rapidly evolving business landscape, organizations leveraging Salesforce face critical decisions regarding their org strategy, primarily whether to opt for a single-org or a multi-org strategy. This decision significantly impacts an organization's ability to innovate, collaborate, and grow.

Choosing an appropriate org strategy requires a comprehensive understanding of various factors, including business objectives, technical requirements, and architectural considerations. For instance, the enterprise architecture operating model is important to consider for an org strategy. By carefully assessing these aspects, organizations can tailor their Salesforce environment strategy to their unique needs.

Fed angle

At the Federal Reserve Bank of Chicago, the org strategy decision is not purely technical — it is a governance and risk management decision. A multi-org approach can enforce data isolation between business lines (e.g., bank supervision vs. payments), while a single-org approach enables a unified audit trail and centralized access control. The enterprise architecture operating model must be assessed before any migration or consolidation is proposed to senior leadership.

Salesforce Org Strategy

Page 6 · Org Strategy Defined

Salesforce Org Strategy

An org strategy is a plan of how to best use Salesforce for the business.

Org Architecture

The underlying org architecture is used in Salesforce solutions and aligns with the business needs to maximize value.

Solutions

It provides a consistent set of processes and procedures for building solutions on the Salesforce platform.

Page 7 · Single-Org vs. Multi-Org Strategy

Single-Org vs. Multi-Org Strategy

When planning a Salesforce implementation, one of the critical decisions is choosing between a single-org and a multi-org strategy.

Impact

The org strategy impacts the scalability, complexity, governance, and overall architecture of the Salesforce environment.

Decision

Choosing the org strategy is not just a technical decision, but one that aligns closely with the business strategies, operational models, and long-term objectives.

Interview angle

When asked about org strategy, lead with the business dimension, not the technical one. Examiners want to hear that the decision flows from operating model, data sharing needs, and regulatory requirements — not from preference or legacy org structure. The technical constraints (limits, Apex design maturity, integration cost) are supporting evidence for a business-driven recommendation.

Page 8 · Org Strategy Considerations

Org Strategy Considerations

When choosing between a single-org and a multi-org strategy, architectural, business and technical considerations should be taken into account.

Architectural

Architectural aspects such as operating model and integration strategy for business processes and data need to be considered.

Business

Business considerations are related to aspects such as funding, control, lines of business, overhead of multiple orgs, and unified view of data.

Technical

Technical considerations are related to change management, data security, compliance, collaboration, technical roles and responsibilities, and technical limitations.

Page 9 · Section

Architectural Considerations

Page 10 · Enterprise Architecture Operating Model

Enterprise Architecture Operating Model

There are four possible enterprise architecture operating models based on the degree of business process integration and business process standardization.

Low Standardization High Standardization
High Integration Coordination Unification
Low Integration Diversification Replication
Memorize the 2x2 matrix: Integration (Y-axis) vs. Standardization (X-axis). Unification = high/high → single-org. Diversification = low/low → multi-org. Coordination and Replication fall in between. This matrix is a frequent exam question anchor.
Page 11 · Diversification

Diversification

The diversification model applies to companies with low business process integration and low business process standardization.

Who

Companies with independent business units with different customers and expertise. Examples: Johnson & Johnson, Carlson Companies, GE.

Key IT Capability

Providing economies of scale without limiting independence.

Org Strategy

Usually have multiple orgs since the consolidation of multiple business processes and data models into one org is very complicated.

Culture

Diversified business units are usually not accustomed to working in the same space as the other lines of business.

Page 12 · Replication

Replication

The replication model applies to companies with low business process integration and high business process standardization.

Who

Companies with independent but similar business units. Examples: Marriott, CEMEX, ING DIRECT.

Key IT Capability

Providing standard infrastructure and application components for global efficiencies.

Org Strategy

Can have multiple orgs. A managed package should be deployed from a central org into all the replicated business units.

Local Control

Local control/administration can be provided while maintaining the predefined standard business processes.

Fed angle

The Federal Reserve System's twelve regional Reserve Banks map well to the Replication model: each bank is operationally independent but follows standardized processes set by the Board of Governors. A managed package deployed from a hub org into regional orgs is a natural architectural fit — standard processes enforced centrally, local administration preserved at each bank.

Page 13 · Coordination

Coordination

The coordination model applies to companies with high business process integration and low business process standardization.

Who

Companies with unique business units with a need to know each other's transactions. Examples: Merrill Lynch, Toyota Motor Marketing Europe, Metlife.

Key IT Capability

Access to shared data, through standard technology interfaces.

Org Strategy

Should have as few orgs as possible. A web services integration strategy can be utilized, but the potential value is much higher in a single org.

Governance

A proper governance strategy and an effective service delivery strategy are necessary to keep the multiple lines of business satisfied.

Page 14 · Unification

Unification

The unification model applies to companies with high business process integration and high business process standardization.

Who

Companies with a single business with global process standards and global data access. Examples: Delta Airlines, Dow Chemical, Pepsi Americas.

Key IT Capability

Enterprise systems reinforcing standard processes and providing global data access.

Org Strategy

Should have as few orgs as possible. This enterprise architecture operating model is the most suitable for a single-org strategy.

Interview angle

The Unification model is the canonical justification for a single-org recommendation. When an exam question presents a company with globally standardized processes and a need for shared customer data, the answer is single-org with a Unification operating model. Conversely, Diversification always points to multi-org. The middle two (Coordination, Replication) require judgment calls based on additional data points.

Page 15 · Integration Strategy

Integration Strategy

It is important to define the strategy for the integration of business processes and data across multiple orgs.

Enterprise Service Bus

Enterprise Service Bus can be considered for a decoupled integration architecture.

Salesforce Connect

Salesforce Connect can be utilized for integration around master data that are only needed for reference.

Hub & Spoke

Hub & spoke architecture is suitable to share master data in a central org with other orgs.

Managed Packages

The use of managed packages can be considered to deploy functionality from the hub org to the spoke orgs.

Reporting Org

All the necessary data can be pushed to a separate org for reporting and/or collaboration.

Fed angle

For the Fed, the Enterprise Service Bus (decoupled) and hub-and-spoke patterns are especially relevant: MuleSoft or a similar ESB sits between Salesforce orgs and core banking systems, ensuring no direct org-to-org data sharing that would bypass audit controls. A separate reporting org also keeps production data read-only for analytics, satisfying least-privilege principles for data access.

Page 16 · Section

Business Considerations

Page 17 · Funding & Scope of Control

Funding & Scope of Control

Funding sources and control scope in a company might dictate the org strategy, making it necessary to emphasize coordination and data architecture awareness.

Coordination

The architect needs to coordinate and be aware of the entire Salesforce community in the company.

Data Objects

The required data objects in each org should be identified. Each org should be aware of any duplicated data objects.

Global CoE

The company should be pushed toward setting up a Global Center of Excellence (CoE).

Reference Architecture

A Reference Architecture should be designed and built by the CoE, defining where and when an additional org is reasonable or necessary.

Architecture Decisions

Architecture decisions should be made at the correct level of the company.

Fed angle

At the Federal Reserve Bank of Chicago, the CoE structure is equivalent to a centralized architecture governance body reporting to the CTO. The Reference Architecture defines the canonical pattern for any new org — and critically, it documents the justification for each additional org, creating an auditable decision trail that change-control audits and technology risk reviews depend on.

Page 18 · Lines of Business (LOBs)

Lines of Business (LOBs)

Having multiple lines of business is not a good reason to have separate orgs. Challenges increase with more lines of business.

Center of Excellence

A CoE should be set up to manage the stakeholders and articulate a clear roadmap of functionality releases across the LOBs.

Delegated Administration

LOBs can be allowed to make their own changes through the use of delegated administration and a tiered data architecture.

License Tax

A license tax can be established to fund development and maintenance resources to support all the LOBs.

LOB Sizing

Smaller LOBs may be pooled around a CoE and centralized resources while the larger LOBs can fund and support their own org.

Key exam gotcha: multiple LOBs alone do NOT justify separate orgs. The justification for a separate org must be data isolation, regulatory compliance, or org limits — not LOB count.
Page 19 · Overhead of Multiple Orgs

Overhead of Multiple Orgs

Costs increase with multiple orgs due to licensing, integration, and management overhead.

Licensing Costs

If users need to access multiple orgs, licensing costs increase.

Third-Party Licensing

Third-party licensing costs also increase depending on the solution.

Integration Costs

Integrating data and business processes across the orgs increases integration costs.

Environment Management

Multiple sandboxes and release cycles in a multi-org design increase the environment management costs.

SSO

The need for SSO becomes necessary to improve user experience and avoid wasting support time.

Interview angle

When making a multi-org recommendation, always acknowledge the overhead explicitly — it shows architectural maturity. A complete answer includes: licensing cost delta, integration complexity budget, the SSO requirement, and the need for multiple sandbox environments across each org's release cycle. Leaving any of these out signals an incomplete cost-of-ownership analysis.

Page 20 · Customer 360 & Global Case Management

Customer 360 & Global Case Management

Having customer 360 or global case management requirements is often the deciding factor when it comes to choosing a single-org strategy.

Single-Org Strategy

Single org strategy is suitable when customer data need to be unified in a single org for a 360-degree view.

Multi-Org Strategy

Multi-org strategy can support these requirements, but with added complexity and cost. A hub/spoke or reporting hub can be utilized.

Fed angle

At the Fed, the equivalent of "Customer 360" is a unified counterparty or institution view — knowing that a supervised bank, a payments counterparty, and a research data contact are the same legal entity. This unified view is a strong driver for consolidation into a single org or a data hub strategy, and is something that examiners and senior leadership will cite as a business value justification for consolidation investment.

Page 21 · Section

Technical Considerations

Page 22 · Complexity of Multiple LOBs

Complexity of Multiple Lines of Business (LOBs)

Designing a single Salesforce org to support multiple lines of business introduces a lot of complexity.

Naming Standards

Good naming standards should be implemented across the data model, configuration design, and custom code.

Security & Sharing

It is difficult to design, maintain, and refactor security and sharing features, including profiles, permission sets, role hierarchy, sharing model, and public groups.

Apex Design

Apex design must be very mature to support a large enterprise model. There should be a strict separation of concerns, and a technical architect should oversee all aspects of the org.

Data Architecture

A data architect should be employed to manage the design and maintenance of the data model, as it can become much more complex and controversial as the scope increases.

Page 23 · Effective Change Management

Effective Change Management

Parallel work streams can make supporting many initiatives at once in a single org difficult. An effective governance model is often required.

Global CoE

The business architecture and common standards of all the Salesforce instances across the enterprise should be managed by a global CoE.

Governance Committee

The governance committee of each org should perform product management and set direction and priority within each org. Roles and responsibilities and a RACI matrix should be defined for changes.

Architectural Review Board

The architectural review board of an org should actively design, review, and approve technical changes to the environment, including configuration, data model, and custom code.

Tiered Administration

Tiered administration teams in each org enable the LOBs to make some changes by themselves without requiring production releases.

Fed angle

The Fed's change management model maps directly here: the Architecture Review Board (ARB) approves changes to data model and custom code; the IT governance committee sets priority and direction; and tiered admin teams (LOB admins) handle configuration changes below a defined risk threshold without production releases. A RACI matrix is a required artifact in the Fed's change-control documentation package — examiners ask for it by name.

Page 24 · Regulatory, Compliance & Security

Regulatory, Compliance, and Security Requirements

Regulatory, compliance, and security requirements may necessitate separate orgs due to the need for data isolation.

Data Isolation

Due to certain factors, data isolation is necessary in some industries like healthcare and financial services.

PII Security

The security requirements may differ for Personally Identifiable Information (PII) than other business data.

Encryption & IP

Complex encryption requirements, IP requirements, and confidential business data of some LOBs may necessitate separate orgs.

Fed angle

This slide is the most directly applicable to the Federal Reserve context. The Fed operates in financial services — one of the two industries named. Data residency requirements (certain supervisory data cannot reside outside the Federal Reserve System boundary), distinct PII handling for bank employees vs. supervised institution contacts, and confidential supervisory information (CSI) classifications can each justify separate orgs. Always cite the specific regulatory driver (FISMA, FR-internal data classification policy) rather than the generic "compliance" catchall.

Page 25 · Chatter

Chatter

If users are spread across multiple orgs, social collaboration can be a big hindrance due to the absence of cross-org Chatter support.

Solutions

Partner solutions and custom development options are available for cross-org collaboration, but they can be very complicated to implement correctly.

Scope

Chatter should not be the main driver for the org strategy, but it is important to understand the scope of social collaboration desired across the enterprise.

Chatter is a consideration, not a decision driver. Never recommend a single-org strategy based primarily on Chatter needs — the complexity and cost of consolidation almost always outweigh Chatter benefits alone.
Page 26 · Development, Administration & Release Management

Development, Administration, and Release Management

Who will modify and maintain orgs is an important consideration for choosing a suitable org strategy.

Multiple Dev Teams

Having multiple development teams in the same org increases overhead and governance complexity.

Environment Strategy

Having multiple teams in the same org requires a robust environment management and release strategy.

Deployment Manager

A dedicated deployment manager should be employed to handle the movement of code and metadata across the environments.

System Administrators

The number of true system administrators should be as few as possible. Using custom profiles and permission sets is critical.

Multi-Org Releases

Multiple orgs may need their own release management processes or complicate existing ones.

Interview angle

The dedicated deployment manager role is an exam favorite. At architect altitude, you should speak to who owns the release pipeline, how metadata is tracked (source control in Git, CI/CD via GitLab or GitHub Actions), and how sandbox refreshes are coordinated across multiple teams. Minimizing true sysadmin count and using permission sets for role-specific access reflects least-privilege, which resonates strongly in a Fed context.

Page 27 · Support & Service Management

Support & Service Management

Different layers of support are necessary to consider for each line of business.

Tier 0 — LOB Support

Each line of business should have its own tier 0 support layer for handling basic support requests.

Tier 1 — Service Desk

There should be a single service desk and entry point for all the tier 1 Salesforce support.

Tier 2 & 3 — Advanced

Each org should have its own tier 2 and 3 support teams to address more complex issues and troubleshooting.

Page 28 · Org Limits

Org Limits

Org limitations may necessitate using multiple orgs.

Fresh Limits

A new org offers a fresh set of limits that can help get around issues that cannot be solved in a single org.

Increasing Limits

Before setting up a new org, Salesforce should be consulted because it may be possible to increase some limits.

Org limits are a valid but last-resort justification for a new org. Always consult Salesforce about limit increases first. Using org limits as the primary driver for multi-org strategy signals incomplete architectural analysis.

Business Scenario 1 — Cosmic Energy

Scenario

Cosmic Energy, a global leader in renewable energy solutions, is navigating through a period of rapid expansion and digital transformation. The company, renowned for its innovative approach to energy management and sustainability, is reevaluating its Salesforce org strategy to better align with its evolving business model, diverse customer base, and sprawling global operations. The company's Salesforce architect is tasked with evaluating the current org strategy and proposing a roadmap that supports the company's business objectives, technical requirements, and architectural vision.

Requirements & Solutions

Page 32 · Requirement (Part 1)

Requirement (Part 1)

Scenario

The company requires suitable org strategies for different situations:

  • Multiple Salesforce orgs are used for different lines of business (LOBs). The CTO wants to achieve a more comprehensive view of its customers by aggregating customer data from these disparate orgs.
  • Several companies with their own Salesforce orgs were recently acquired and merged as new business units. They should have standardized business processes, but business process integration is not required.
Page 33 · Requirement (Part 2)

Requirement (Part 2)

Scenario

  • One of the newly acquired companies in Asia has a different sales process and needs to comply strictly with the local laws and regulations.
  • The company wants to launch a new line of business for geothermal energy solutions in New Zealand that will have independent processes.
Page 34 · Solution (Part 1)

Solution (Part 1)

Solution

For a comprehensive view of customer data across multiple orgs, a Hub-and-Spoke multi-org strategy should be utilized. This involves consolidating customer data into a central org (hub) that aggregates information from other orgs (spokes). This centralized data hub ensures a unified customer view across different LOBs, facilitating better analytics, personalized customer engagement, and streamlined reporting without the need for complete process integration across all units.

Interview angle

Hub-and-spoke is the exam pattern for "unified customer view without full consolidation." Articulate clearly what the hub does (aggregates, reports, provides 360-degree view) vs. what the spokes do (operate with LOB-specific processes). The hub org typically does not own transactional processes — it is a data aggregation and reporting layer.

Page 35 · Solution (Part 2)

Solution (Part 2)

Solution

For business units acquired with their own Salesforce orgs, where standardized business processes are crucial but integration between these processes is not necessary, a multi-org strategy should be utilized. A managed package or a set of managed packages can be developed to enforce standardized business processes across all the orgs. This would ensure consistency in operations while allowing each unit to operate independently in their Salesforce environment.

Fed angle

Managed packages for cross-org process standardization map directly to the Fed's model for shared services. A managed package deployed from a hub (the CoE org) into regional or functional orgs enforces baseline controls (naming standards, required fields, approval processes) while allowing local configuration. This is the architectural equivalent of a policy mandate with local implementation flexibility — a familiar governance pattern at the Fed.

Page 36 · Solution (Part 3)

Solution (Part 3)

Solution

A separate org should be set up for the newly acquired company in Asia. Given the unique sales process and stringent local compliance requirements, maintaining a separate Salesforce org for this unit would allow for tailored configurations, localization, and adherence to local laws. This autonomy would ensure that the business unit can operate effectively within its regulatory environment while allowing for innovations specific to the regional market.

Fed angle

Local regulatory compliance as a justification for a separate org is directly applicable to the Fed. Confidential supervisory information (CSI) handling requirements, data residency mandates, and jurisdiction-specific data protection rules (analogous to Asia's local laws in this scenario) are legitimate, documented reasons to maintain org isolation. Always capture the regulatory citation in the Reference Architecture decision record.

Page 37 · Solution (Part 4)

Solution (Part 4)

Solution

For the new geothermal energy solutions line of business in New Zealand, which requires independent processes, a stand-alone org strategy is the most suitable. This approach would allow the new business unit to develop and implement processes that are unique to its operations and market needs without being constrained by the existing org structures or processes. A stand-alone org would provide the flexibility needed to innovate and rapidly adapt to the unique challenges and opportunities of the geothermal energy market.

Stand-alone org is distinct from the hub-and-spoke spoke org: a stand-alone org is fully independent and not integrated back into any hub. Choose stand-alone when there is no customer data sharing requirement and no need for process standardization with existing orgs.

Scenario

Cosmic Technologies, a leading innovator in the high-tech manufacturing sector, is undergoing significant organizational changes and technological advancements. Page 40 · Section

Requirements & Solutions

Page 41 · Requirement (Part 1)

Requirement (Part 1)

Scenario

The company has multiple lines of business (LOBs) with different orgs and processes but shares global customers. It has implemented a multi-org strategy with one org consolidating the customer data for a 360-degree view. However, since it wants to use Chatter to facilitate collaboration among the existing lines of business, it would like to switch to a single org despite the complexity of having multiple lines of business in a single org. The architect needs to provide the benefits and challenges of transitioning from a multi-org to a single-org strategy.

Page 42 · Requirement (Part 2)

Requirement (Part 2)

Scenario

In addition, it is launching a new line of business that does not share customers with the existing lines of business and needs to implement unique business processes. A suitable org strategy is required for this new business unit.

Page 43 · Solution (Part 1)

Solution (Part 1)

Solution

Switching from a multi-org to a single-org strategy has various benefits, including reduced administration overhead, improved collaboration among the different lines of business, and increased simplicity of creating a 360-degree view of customer data. However, certain challenges need to be considered. Apex design must be very mature to support a large enterprise model. There should be a strict separation of concerns, and a technical architect should oversee all aspects of the org. Managing the design and maintenance of the data model is important, as it can become much more complex and controversial as the scope increases.

Interview angle

This is a classic "benefits vs. challenges" exam answer frame. Memorize the three benefits (reduced admin overhead, improved collaboration, simpler 360 view) and the three challenges (Apex maturity requirement, security/sharing complexity, data model governance). The exam will often ask you to identify which of these applies to a given scenario — knowing both sides equally well is what separates an architect answer from a developer answer.

Page 44 · Solution (Part 2)

Solution (Part 2)

Solution

For the new line of business that does not share its customers with others, a new standalone Salesforce org should be used. It should not be integrated with the other org due to its unique business processes.

The two-condition test for a standalone org: (1) no shared customers/data with existing orgs, AND (2) unique business processes that would be constrained by existing org structures. Both conditions must be true. If there is any customer data sharing, a hub-and-spoke or integration pattern is needed instead.

Cheatsheet — System Design: Org Strategy

Enterprise Architecture Operating Model → Org Strategy

Model Integration Standardization Org Strategy Key IT Capability
Unification High High Single-org (fewest orgs possible) Enterprise systems reinforcing standard processes & global data access
Coordination High Low As few orgs as possible; single-org preferred Shared data via standard technology interfaces
Replication Low High Multi-org with managed package from hub Standard infrastructure & application components for global efficiencies
Diversification Low Low Multi-org (consolidation too complicated) Economies of scale without limiting independence

Org Strategy Pattern Matching

Situation Recommended Strategy Key Justification
Need unified 360° customer view, single process Single-org Unification model; Customer 360 driver
Multiple LOBs, shared customers, no process integration needed Hub-and-spoke multi-org Data aggregation without full consolidation
Acquired orgs needing standardized processes, no integration Multi-org + managed package Replication model; enforce standards without merging
Regulatory/compliance data isolation required Separate org Data isolation mandate; regulatory driver
New LOB, no shared customers, unique processes Stand-alone org No integration need; independent processes
Org limits blocking growth New org (last resort) Consult Salesforce for limit increase first

Multi-Org Overhead Checklist

  • Licensing: User and third-party license costs multiply per org.
  • Integration: Each org boundary requires an integration pattern (ESB, Salesforce Connect, API).
  • Environments: Each org needs its own sandbox refresh cadence and release pipeline.
  • SSO: Mandatory to prevent fragmented login experiences across orgs.
  • Support: Each org needs tier 2/3 support teams; one shared tier 1 service desk.
  • Governance: Global CoE + per-org governance committee + per-org ARB.

High-Yield Exam Gotchas

  • Multiple LOBs alone do NOT justify separate orgs — the correct answer is delegated administration, tiered data architecture, and a CoE within a single org.
  • Chatter is never the primary driver for org strategy — it is a consideration, not a justification for consolidation.
  • Org limits are a last resort — always ask Salesforce to increase limits before creating a new org.
  • Unification = single-org; Diversification = multi-org. Coordination and Replication require judgment.
  • Hub-and-spoke ≠ single-org: the hub aggregates data from spokes but each spoke retains its own org; it is still a multi-org architecture.
  • Managed packages enforce standardization across orgs in the Replication model — they deploy from the hub to all spoke orgs.
  • Before recommending org consolidation, verify Apex design maturity — immature Apex in a large enterprise single-org is a high-risk pattern.
  • True sysadmin count should be minimized — use custom profiles and permission sets for LOB-level access.
  • A Reference Architecture built by the CoE must document every justification for an additional org — this is the auditable decision record.

Fed angle — Tying it together

For a Federal Reserve Bank of Chicago platform engineer, the org strategy decision intersects governance, data residency, and regulatory compliance at every step. The most important principles to carry into the interview: (1) Regulatory data isolation (CSI, PII classification differences) is a legitimate and documented justification for separate orgs — cite the specific policy. (2) A Global CoE with a Reference Architecture is non-negotiable in a regulated environment — it is the governance artifact that change-control audits and technology risk reviews will ask for. (3) The RACI matrix and ARB approval process are the control mechanisms that satisfy examiners when reviewing org-level architecture changes. (4) Any consolidation from multi-org to single-org must include a mature GitLab CI/CD pipeline, dedicated deployment manager, and documented release impact assessment before each production deployment. The complexity costs of consolidation are real — demonstrate that you have priced them in.
